Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 21, 2025

Welcome to zulkaif.com ("Website"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you visit or interact with this Website.

This Website is a personal portfolio intended to showcase projects, skills, experience, and professional work.

By using the Website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of the Website.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Voluntarily Provide

zulkaif.com does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to browse the Website.

If you voluntarily contact the website owner, for example by email or through a contact form, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Message or inquiry
  • Any other information you choose to include in your communication

This information is used only for purposes reasonably related to responding to your inquiry, communicating with you, or providing information you have requested.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your inquiry.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by the Website, its hosting provider, analytics services, or other third-party technologies.

Depending on the services in use, this information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Device type
  • Internet service provider
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate geographic location derived from IP address
  • Interaction and usage information
  • Technical diagnostic information

This information may be used for security, troubleshooting, website administration, analytics, performance monitoring, and improving the Website.

2. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable, personal information may be processed on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Consent: where you have given consent to a particular processing activity.
  • Legitimate interests: where processing is reasonably necessary for purposes such as maintaining, securing, analyzing, and improving the Website, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Contractual necessity: where processing is necessary to take steps at your request or fulfill an agreement.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law or a valid legal request.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time, subject to applicable law.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

zulkaif.com may use cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that can help websites remember preferences, maintain functionality, understand website usage, and improve performance.

Cookies may include:

Essential Cookies

These may be necessary for basic website functionality, security, or technical operation.

Analytics Cookies

If analytics services are enabled, these cookies may help understand how visitors interact with the Website, such as which pages are visited and how the Website is used.

Preference Cookies

These may remember settings or preferences to improve your browsing experience.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Where required by applicable law, non-essential cookies may be used only after obtaining appropriate consent.

Disabling cookies may affect certain Website functionality.

4. Analytics and Third-Party Services

The Website may use third-party services, including analytics, hosting, security, content delivery, or other technical services.

These providers may process certain information about visitors as necessary to provide their services.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and data-processing practices. Where applicable, you should review the privacy policies of the relevant providers.

zulkaif.com does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. External Links

The Website may contain links to websites, applications, social media profiles, repositories, or other third-party resources.

Once you leave zulkaif.com, this Privacy Policy no longer governs information collected by the third-party website or service. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

You should review the privacy practices of external websites before providing them with personal information.

6. How Information Is Used

Information collected through or in connection with the Website may be used for purposes including:

  • Responding to inquiries and communications
  • Providing information requested by visitors
  • Operating and maintaining the Website
  • Improving Website content and functionality
  • Understanding Website traffic and usage
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing security issues
  • Preventing abuse, fraud, or unauthorized activity
  • Troubleshooting technical problems
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations
  • Protecting the rights, property, and security of the website owner and visitors

Personal information will not be used for materially incompatible purposes without appropriate notice or a lawful basis where required.

7. Data Retention

Personal information will generally be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, respond to communications, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with legal obligations.

The actual retention period may vary depending on the nature of the information and the circumstances in which it was collected.

Technical logs and analytics information may be retained according to the policies and configurations of the relevant hosting or analytics providers.

8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Personal information may be disclosed in limited circumstances, including:

  • To service providers that help operate, secure, host, analyze, or maintain the Website
  • Where you have requested or authorized the disclosure
  • When required by applicable law, regulation, court order, or valid legal process
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, property, or security of the Website, its owner, or others
  • In connection with a business transfer or restructuring, where applicable

Service providers may process information only as reasonably necessary to provide their services and may be subject to contractual or legal obligations regarding data protection.

9. International Data Transfers

Some third-party service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country in which you are located.

Where applicable data-protection laws require safeguards for international transfers, appropriate safeguards may be used, such as contractual protections or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply to you, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Object to certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request a copy of certain personal information in a portable format
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data-protection authority

These rights are subject to applicable legal limitations and exceptions.

To exercise a privacy right, contact the website owner using the contact information provided on zulkaif.com.

11. GDPR Rights for Individuals in the EEA, UK, and Similar Jurisdictions

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with similar data-protection rights, you may have additional rights under applicable law.

These may include rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to, or obtain portability of your personal information.

Where applicable, you may also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection supervisory authority.

The availability and scope of these rights depend on the applicable law and the circumstances of the processing.

12. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident and applicable California privacy law applies to the Website, you may have certain rights concerning your personal information.

Depending on applicable law, these may include the right to:

  • Know or access categories and specific pieces of personal information collected about you
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Obtain information about the categories of personal information collected, used, or disclosed
  • Opt out of certain sales or sharing of personal information, where applicable
  • Limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, where applicable
  • Receive equal treatment for exercising applicable privacy rights

zulkaif.com does not intentionally sell personal information for monetary consideration. The Website also does not intentionally use personal information for targeted advertising unless this policy is updated to disclose such practices.

If you wish to exercise an applicable California privacy right, contact the website owner using the contact information provided on zulkaif.com.

Where required by law, reasonable steps may be taken to verify requests before fulfilling them.

13. Do Not Track and Similar Signals

Some browsers and devices provide "Do Not Track" or similar privacy signals.

Because there is currently no universally consistent technical standard for responding to all such signals, the Website may not respond to every browser-based Do Not Track setting.

Where applicable law requires recognition of a legally recognized opt-out preference signal, the Website will process such signals in accordance with applicable requirements.

14. Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures may be used to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no Internet transmission, website, server, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should therefore understand that transmission of information to or through the Website is undertaken at your own risk.

15. Children's Privacy

zulkaif.com is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Where applicable law establishes a higher minimum age for consent or imposes additional requirements, those requirements may apply.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Website, please contact the website owner. Reasonable steps will be taken to investigate and delete such information where appropriate.

16. Sensitive Personal Information

The Website does not intentionally request or require sensitive personal information for ordinary browsing.

Please do not submit sensitive information, such as government identification numbers, financial account information, passwords, health information, or other highly sensitive data through the Website unless specifically requested and necessary.

17. Third-Party Privacy Policies

This Privacy Policy applies only to zulkaif.com.

Third-party websites, services, analytics providers, hosting providers, and other external services may have separate privacy policies. zulkaif.com is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third parties.

You are encouraged to review the applicable privacy policies of third-party services before using them.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in the Website, technology, third-party services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated Last updated date.

Your continued use of the Website after an updated Privacy Policy is posted constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

19. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise an applicable privacy right, or have a privacy-related concern, you may contact the website owner through the contact information provided on zulkaif.com.

When making a privacy request, please provide enough information to allow the request to be understood and processed. Additional information may be requested where reasonably necessary to verify your identity or protect against unauthorized disclosure.
